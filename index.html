Process

Three steps to a verdict

Every upload goes through the same forensic pipeline, whether it's a 10-second clip or a full interview.

📤
01

Upload media

Drop in a video or audio file, or paste a link. Clarifake extracts frames, waveforms, and metadata automatically.

🔎
02

Analyze signals

Multiple independent models check for generation artifacts — spectral noise, lip-sync drift, unnatural blinking, and more.

📊
03

Get a confidence score

Signals are combined into a single 0–100 score and a clear verdict: authentic, synthetic, or inconclusive.
